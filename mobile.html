Read with care
  • The report time is when someone filed the ticket, not when the dumping happened.
  • 311 locations are often snapped to an intersection or street centerline, so a strict cone misses some real sightings.
  • Camera positions and directions are crowd-sourced on OpenStreetMap (the data DeFlock maps). Field of view and range are not recorded, so they are the sliders above.
  • Retention varies by agency. Days left assumes the 30-day Flock default; older reports show "gone".
  • Size is estimated from the reporter's answers and description where the ticket system has them (SeeClickFix). Other reports only have their category.
  • Reports are clipped to the outlined area. Cameras up to 200 m outside it are kept, since they can see across the line.
